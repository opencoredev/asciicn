## Usage

```tsx
import { SacredTorusScene } from "@/components/asciicn/scenes/sacred-torus-scene";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate h-[560px]">
      <SacredTorusScene className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
    </section>
  );
}
```

The knot is a real 3D mesh: a tube of 3,080 vertices swept along a (p, q) torus knot, projected in perspective and rasterised into a depth buffer at the charset's sample resolution. Each sample is lit with a diffuse term, a tight highlight and a rim, and coloured by a hue that shifts with the surface normal like oil on water. Every `hold` seconds the knot eases into the next one in `knots`.

With ramp charsets the outline is drawn with `- / | \` picked from the screen-space normal wherever the surface turns away, so the tube reads as a drawing rather than a blur. Three wireframe polyhedra orbit it and pass behind it, hidden by the same depth buffer.

Drag to turn the knot. Hovering tilts it toward the pointer.

## Examples

### Braille in vapor

Braille shades the tube with ordered dot patterns, so the light falls off in dot density, and the polyhedra become one-dot lines. The `vapor` duotone ramp runs from violet shadow to pink highlights, and a thicker `tube` fills more of the frame.

### A still trefoil on your page

One knot in `knots` stops the morphing. Without solids or sigil, with `background="transparent"` and mono ink, it reads as a classic ASCII donut in your theme's colours. The dense ramp gives it 70 levels of shading.

## Notes

- The canvas is `aria-hidden`; describe the visual in text nearby if it carries meaning.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` and with `animate={false}` it draws one frame at `stillTime`.
- The mesh is rebuilt and rasterised on the CPU every frame with buffers allocated once per size. Braille samples at 2x4 per cell, so it does eight times the raster work of a ramp; at 200x60 cells that is still a few milliseconds a frame.
- p and q with a common factor (such as 2 and 4) trace a closed loop twice, which looks like a plain torus.
