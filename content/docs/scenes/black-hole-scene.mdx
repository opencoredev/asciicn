## Usage

```tsx
import { BlackHoleScene } from "@/components/asciicn/scenes/black-hole-scene";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[520px]">
      <BlackHoleScene className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1 className="text-white">Event horizon</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

Every sample traces a light ray backwards from the camera and bends it, step by step, with the equation for light around a non-spinning black hole. Nothing in the picture is painted on: the part of the disk behind the hole appears as an arch over the shadow and a second, smaller arch under it because that is where its light ends up. Stars near the edge smear into arcs for the same reason.

The disk runs from the innermost stable orbit, three times the horizon radius, out to about thirteen. Its streaks are noise laid out along each orbit, and inner rings turn faster than outer ones, as orbits do. Matter on the side coming toward you is brighter and bluer, the side moving away dimmer and redder. Just outside the shadow sits the photon ring, a thin line of light that went around the hole before escaping, brighter on the approaching side.

Moving the pointer up and down raises and lowers the camera; left and right roll the view a little. `tilt` sets the resting height, and `doppler` softens the brightness difference between the two sides.

## Examples

### Edge on, behind a headline

A `tilt` of 3 degrees flattens the disk into one bright line across the shadow, with the lensed far side standing up behind it. The text sits in the dark sky below.

### Dense glyphs through the ember ramp

`colorMode="duotone"` reads each tone through the `ember` ramp instead of the disk's own colours, and the 70-glyph `dense` charset turns the streaks into fine texture. A higher `tilt` opens the disk up; a faster `spin` winds it quicker.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- With reduced motion turned on it draws one still frame, at `stillTime`, and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden, and gives up its WebGL context when far offscreen. Without WebGL2 it shows `fallback`, a gradient of the shadow and disk.
- This is the heaviest of these scenes: each sample takes up to 100 integration steps, so the cost grows with the number of samples. Braille samples eight times per glyph; a larger `cell`, a single-sample charset such as `standard`, or a lower `fps` cuts the work. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
- The physics is simplified where it would hurt the picture: the hole does not spin, the disk is thin, and the Doppler shift is softened so the receding side stays visible in glyphs.
