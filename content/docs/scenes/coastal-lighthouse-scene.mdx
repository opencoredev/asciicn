## Usage

```tsx
import { CoastalLighthouseScene } from "@/components/asciicn/scenes/coastal-lighthouse-scene";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[520px]">
      <CoastalLighthouseScene className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1 className="text-white">Keep the light on</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

By default it draws in Braille with ordered dithering, so the fog, the moon's halo and the beams show as finer or sparser dots instead of a hard cut at one tone.

A striped lighthouse stands on a headland to the right, with the keeper's cottage beside it and one warm window. Two beams leave the lantern in opposite directions and turn. Each beam is a cone in 3D, so it shortens as it swings toward you or away, and the light it scatters is brightest where it crosses the most fog. When a beam points at the camera the lantern flares, with a flat lens streak across the screen. Beams pass behind the tower and the cliff, not through them.

The sea is a swell of several wave trains rolling toward you, with chop on top. The moon, behind two decks of drifting cloud, lays a path of glitter on the water, and clouds near it get silver edges. Foam gathers on the crests and churns where the swell meets the foot of the cliff. Rain slants through and mist lies low over the water.

Moving the pointer shifts the headland, the far shore and the moon by different amounts, and turns the lamp a little ahead of or behind its sweep.

## Examples

### ANSI blocks on amber phosphor

`phosphor="p3"` switches to one amber ink, and `charset="blocks"` draws the scene in the five shade blocks of old DOS art, so the fog and the beams become bands of `░▒▓`. A higher `storm` thickens the cloud and the rain.

### A calm night

`storm={0}` clears the rain and most of the cloud, a low `swell` flattens the sea so the moon's path is a clean line, and a slower `beamSpeed` lets each sweep linger. The `dense` charset gives the water a finer grain.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- With reduced motion turned on it draws one still frame, at `stillTime`, with both beams across the sky, and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden, and gives up its WebGL context when far offscreen. Without WebGL2 it shows `fallback`, a gradient of the moonlit sky and sea.
- The scene is one GLSL function evaluated once per sample, with no loops over the beams or waves beyond a fixed handful. Braille samples eight times per glyph, so it costs more than single-sample charsets on large screens. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
