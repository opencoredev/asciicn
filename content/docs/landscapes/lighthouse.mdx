## Usage

```tsx
import { Lighthouse } from "@/components/asciicn/lighthouse";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[480px]">
      <Lighthouse intensity={0.7} className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1>Your headline</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

A white lighthouse stands on a rocky headland at blue hour. Its lantern room glows amber with a soft halo that breathes slowly, about every eleven seconds. A keeper's cottage beside it keeps one window lit. Below, the headland drops to the sea in a craggy cliff, with a sea stack off its foot.

The sky runs from a peach afterglow low on the left, through rose and violet, to deep blue with the first stars. Long thin clouds catch the afterglow on their undersides and drift slowly right. A low far shore sits hazy on the horizon. The sea mirrors the sky, pale at the horizon and dark near you, and carries the lamp's light as a warm broken path. Slow swells roll in and break into soft foam on the rocks.

Every cell is painted. The default `dots` charset draws each one as a halftone dot whose size follows the brightness, from a faint `·` in the night sky and the deep water to a full `●` in the lamp, the tower and the afterglow. `square` cells, on by default, are as wide as they are tall, so the dots lie on an even lattice like a printed halftone screen; turn it off and the rows sit twice as far apart as the columns. In `truecolor`, the default, each dot takes the scene's own colour on its own night, so the scene looks the same on light and dark pages. `mono` with a `phosphor` and `duotone` with a `ramp` read it by tone alone. `braille` and `quadrants` light a dot only above half tone, so they keep the bright shapes and drop the dark sea and sky; use them with `square={false}`.

On wide boxes the tower stands right of centre, so a headline fits in the middle. On tall boxes it moves nearer the middle and keeps its size.

`intensity` quiets the scene for text on top: colours dim and dots shrink together.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- It ignores the pointer: no parallax and no hover effect.
- With reduced motion turned on, it draws one still frame at `stillTime` and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden, and releases its WebGL context when far offscreen. Without WebGL it shows a flat gradient.
- The whole scene is one shader pass on the GPU, one sample per cell in `dots`. At the default 7px square cell a 1440px wide box is about 200 columns. `braille` samples eight times per cell and `quadrants` four. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
