## Usage

```tsx
import { BalloonValley } from "@/components/asciicn/balloon-valley";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[480px]">
      <BalloonValley intensity={0.85} className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1>Your headline</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

The sun is coming up behind the farthest ridge, its disc half over the line, with a wide warm glow round it. Four ridges step down towards you and open in a V under the sun. Each is darker than the one behind it, with a lit rim along its crest and haze below it, and mist pools in the low ground of every V. At the bottom, two dark hills frame the valley floor, where the river winds towards you catching the dawn and the sun's glitter.

Five hot-air balloons hang in the sky at different depths. The nearest is large and striped red and gold, lit from the sun's side and glowing through its shaded side. The far ones are smaller and fade into the haze. Two of them rise and sink over a few minutes, and the low one drops behind the ridges and comes back up.

Every cell is a dot. The dot grows with the brightness of its colour, so the dark foreground is a field of small dim dots and the sun is drawn in large bright ones. `bayer4` dithering, the default, mixes neighbouring dot sizes so gradients stay smooth. `square` cells, on by default, lay the dots on an even lattice like a halftone screen, about 200 columns across a 1440px screen at the default 7px cell. In `truecolor`, the default, the scene paints its own sky on a deep indigo paper, so it looks the same in light and dark themes. `duotone` with a `ramp` and `mono` with a `phosphor` read the same scene by tone alone.

The motion is slow: mist drifts along the valley, the last stars twinkle, the river shimmers and its glow breathes, and the balloons bob. Nothing reacts to the pointer.

On wide boxes the sun sits a little right of centre and the balloons keep to the sides and the top, leaving the middle clear for a headline. On tall boxes the ridges close in round the sun and the balloons gather in the upper sky.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- It ignores the pointer: no parallax and no hover effect.
- With reduced motion turned on, it draws one still frame at `stillTime` and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden, and releases its WebGL context when far offscreen. Without WebGL it shows a flat dawn gradient.
- The whole valley is one shader pass on the GPU, one sample per cell in `dots`. Mist and ridge texture use a few octaves of noise, so it costs a little more than a flat gradient. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
