## Usage

```tsx
import { RiceTerraces } from "@/components/asciicn/rice-terraces";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[480px]">
      <RiceTerraces intensity={0.6} className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1>Your headline</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

A terraced hillside fills the foreground. Its crest is high on the left and falls away to the right, with a line of trees along the top. The terraces run almost level, bow down over a spur and thin out as they climb, so the near ones are wide and the far ones narrow. Each is a dark earthen wall with a flooded pool behind it. The pools mirror the sky at the angle they see it: the near ones the rose overhead, the far ones the gold at the horizon. So the hill reads as stacked ribbons of light.

Behind it a second terraced hill sits back in the haze, and mist fills the valley at its foot, warming to gold under the sun. Three ridges step back into lavender haze, each lighter than the one in front. The sun stands just above them in a soft glow.

Every cell carries a dot. The `dots` charset draws `·`, `•` and `●`, so the dot's size carries its tone: walls and the night sky overhead are fine points, the ridges are mid dots, and the water, mist and sun are full dots. In `truecolor`, the default, each dot takes its own colour.

The motion is slow. Mist drifts through the valley, the pools shimmer, the sun glitters on the water below it, a few last stars twinkle, and one pale egret glides across the sky every few minutes.

On wide boxes the sun sits right of centre, so the middle stays calm for a headline. On tall boxes it stands in the middle, with the hill on the left and the terraces across the bottom.

The scene paints its own night-blue paper in `truecolor`, `ansi16` and `duotone`, so it looks the same on light and dark pages. `mono` with a `phosphor` reads it by tone alone.

`intensity` dims the scene: colours fade in step with it, and the dots shrink more slowly.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- It ignores the pointer: no parallax and no hover effect.
- With reduced motion turned on, it draws one still frame at `stillTime` and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden, and releases its WebGL context when far offscreen. Without WebGL it shows a flat dawn gradient.
- The whole scene is one shader pass on the GPU, one sample per cell in `dots`. `braille` samples eight times per cell and `quadrants` four. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
