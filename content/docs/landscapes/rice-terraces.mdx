## Usage

```tsx
import { RiceTerraces } from "@/components/asciicn/rice-terraces";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[480px]">
      <RiceTerraces intensity={0.8} className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1>Your headline</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

A terraced hillside fills the middle ground. Its crest is high on the left and falls away to the right, with a line of trees along the top. The terraces wrap the hill: they climb with the crest, bow toward you over each spur and tuck back into each gully, and no two are the same width. Each level is a flooded field, then the dark earthen retaining wall of the level above, then that wall's top edge, where grass catches the low sun in green-gold. Bunds split each level into separate fields. Each field mirrors the sky at its own angle, blue near you and silver farther up, darkens along its far edge where it reflects the wall above, and glitters where it lies under the sun. A few fields are planted with young rice instead.

In front of it a darker ridge crosses the bottom of the frame, with a few trees and tufts of grass in silhouette against the fields. Behind it a second terraced hill sits back in the haze, and mist fills the valley at its foot. Three ridges step back, each with less detail, lower contrast and smaller dots than the one in front, and their crests dissolve into a slate and silver sky. The sun stands high on the right in a soft cream glow.

Most of the frame is quiet. The sky is sparse specks, thinning toward the zenith. Walls, shadowed faces and the front ridge are fine points only a little lighter than the paper. Large bright dots are kept for the sun, its glitter, the lit bank tops and the near fields, so near and far separate at a glance. The `dots` charset draws `·`, `•` and `●`, so a dot's size carries its tone, and in `truecolor`, the default, each dot takes its own colour. Cells are square by default, 7px each way, so the dots lie on an even lattice like a halftone screen. `square={false}` uses the font's tall cells instead.

The motion is slow. Mist drifts through the valley, the fields shimmer, the sun glitters on the water below it, a few last stars twinkle, and one pale egret glides across the sky every few minutes.

The middle of the frame is mist and far ridges in low contrast, so a centred headline sits on calm ground. On wide boxes the sun stands in the upper right; on tall boxes it rises above the headline, with the terraces below it.

The scene paints its own deep slate paper in `truecolor`, `ansi16` and `duotone`, so it looks the same on light and dark pages. `mono` with a `phosphor` reads it by tone alone.

`intensity` dims the scene: colours fade in step with it, and the dots shrink more slowly.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- It ignores the pointer: no parallax and no hover effect.
- With reduced motion turned on, it draws one still frame at `stillTime` and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden, and releases its WebGL context when far offscreen. Without WebGL it shows a flat dawn gradient.
- The whole scene is one shader pass on the GPU, one sample per cell in `dots`. `braille` samples eight times per cell and `quadrants` four. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
