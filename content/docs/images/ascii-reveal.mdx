## Usage

```tsx
import { AsciiReveal } from "@/components/asciicn/ascii-reveal";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <AsciiReveal
      src="/photos/launch.jpg"
      alt="The rocket on the pad at dawn"
      direction="down"
      className="w-full max-w-xl"
    />
  );
}
```

`AsciiReveal` starts as the picture drawn in glyphs. It samples the photo with the same area-averaging sampler as `AsciiImage`, so every glyph is the average of the pixels under its cell.

When the reveal runs, an edge sweeps across the picture. Cells at the edge flicker through glyphs in the `accent` colour, then lock in as the photo. Behind the edge the real photo shows. Ahead of it the glyphs hold. The edge bends a little and has some grain, so cells hand over one by one instead of in straight lines.

`trigger` sets what starts it:

- `view` reveals once, when the image scrolls into view.
- `hover` reveals on hover or keyboard focus, and goes back to glyphs on leave. On a touch screen a tap toggles it.
- `manual` follows the `revealed` prop, so you can tie it to your own state.

The box keeps the image's aspect ratio. Pass `ratio` to reserve the space before the image loads, or set your own `aspect-*` class.

## Notes

- The real `<img>` is always in the page and carries `alt`, so screen readers read the image in either state. The glyph layers are `aria-hidden`.
- The image loads lazily. The glyphs sample it once the box comes within about 300 px of the viewport, and the `<img>` uses `loading="lazy"`.
- With reduced motion, or `animate={false}`, there is no sweep. A `view` reveal shows the photo straight away. A `hover` reveal shows the glyphs until you hover or focus it, then swaps to the photo. A `manual` reveal shows whatever `revealed` says.
- The sweep is the only motion. Before it starts and after it ends the component draws nothing new until a prop or the box size changes. Offscreen and in a hidden tab the sweep pauses where it is.
- During the sweep the photo is drawn through a mask of one pixel per cell, so the handover lines up with the glyph grid. At rest the `<img>` itself shows.
- With `trigger="hover"` the box is focusable. If it sits inside a link or a button, use `trigger="manual"` and drive `revealed` from that element's hover and focus instead.
- The sample photos are public domain, from NASA, the Hubble Heritage Team and Ansel Adams for the US National Park Service, all from Wikimedia Commons.
