## Usage

```tsx
import { London } from "@/components/asciicn/london";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[480px]">
      <London intensity={0.7} className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1>Your headline</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

The Elizabeth Tower stands on the far bank of the Thames with its clock face lit. The minute hand goes round once every two minutes, so you can see it move. The Houses of Parliament run upstream from it: a long front of lit windows under a row of pinnacles, the Central Tower's spire and the square Victoria Tower at the end. Downstream, Tower Bridge shows its two towers, high walkways and suspension chains, paler because it sits deeper in the fog.

In front, a green bridge crosses the river on low arches, with three-lantern lamp standards over each pier. A red double-decker drives across it with both decks lit. Fog banks drift through every layer, thicker on the far ones, and each lamp wears an amber halo. The lamps on the embankment drop long streaks into the water, and the tower and Parliament show in the ripples.

On wide boxes the tower sits right of centre, so a headline fits on the left. On tall boxes it stands in the middle. Moving the pointer slides the near layers further than the far ones.

It draws in `standard` glyphs by default. `braille` gives the finest detail, `blocks` and `dense` solid shapes, and `edge` an outline drawing. In `truecolor`, the default, it paints amber lamps in grey-blue fog on a dark night blue. `mono` with a `phosphor` and `duotone` with a `ramp` read the same scene by tone alone.

## Examples

### Behind a headline

`intensity={0.6}` thins the glyphs so white text on the left reads first, and `fog={0.7}` thickens the fog banks a little.

### Sodium duotone

`colorMode="duotone"` with `ramp="sodium"` runs every tone through one amber ramp, from shadow to lamp flame. `braille` glyphs keep the fine detail in a small box, `grade={{ gamma: 1.7 }}` lifts the stone out of the dark end of the ramp, and thick fog with no bus keeps it quiet.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- With reduced motion turned on, it draws one still frame at `stillTime`, with the bus on the bridge, and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden, and releases its WebGL context when far offscreen. Without WebGL it shows a gradient in the same colours.
- The whole city is one shader pass on the GPU. `braille` samples eight times per cell, so it costs the most. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
