## Usage

```tsx
import { SanFrancisco } from "@/components/asciicn/san-francisco";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[480px]">
      <SanFrancisco intensity={0.6} className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1>Your headline</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

The view is from the ocean side at night, looking in through the Golden Gate. The bridge fills the middle: two towers in International Orange, each with four stepped portals above the deck and X bracing below it, standing on concrete piers. The main cable runs from one anchorage up over both towers and dips almost to the road at mid-span, with a suspender every few columns. Under the deck runs the stiffening truss, with amber lamps along the rail. The Marin Headlands rise on the left, and the downtown skyline stands far off on the right, with the Transamerica Pyramid and its spire and the Salesforce Tower with its lit crown.

Nothing built moves, and the pointer has no effect. What changes is slow: a few cars cross the deck as single dots of light, white one way and red the other. The red beacons on the tower tops pulse once every 1.8 seconds. City windows switch on and off a few at a time. The stars brighten and dim, the reflections in the water break and rejoin, and a low fog bank lies behind the bridge on the Marin side, drifting a few box widths an hour.

The towers move apart as the box gets wider and keep their height, so a phone screen still shows the whole span. Edges, cables and suspenders are snapped to the sample grid, so they come out as clean lines of glyphs.

It draws in `quadrants` by default, at a 9px cell. `braille` gives finer lines, and `standard` and `dense` draw the same scene in letters and symbols. In `truecolor` mode, also the default, the bridge is orange against a dark sky; on a light paper the colours are deepened so they still read. In `mono` mode every glyph takes the ink or a `phosphor`, and in `duotone` mode the scene runs through a `ramp` such as `ember` or `glacier`.

`fog` sets how high the bank rises against the headlands, from none at 0. `traffic` sets how many cars cross, up to six. `intensity` thins every glyph at once. Lower it to around 0.5 under a headline so the text on top stays easy to read.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- With reduced motion turned on, it draws one still frame at `stillTime` and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden. Without WebGL it shows a CSS gradient in the scene's colours.
- The whole scene is drawn on the GPU in one pass. In `braille` and `quadrants` each cell takes the colour of its brightest dot, so a cable or suspender one dot thick stays orange. The colour pass reads every dot of the cell to do that, so these two charsets cost more per frame than the others. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
