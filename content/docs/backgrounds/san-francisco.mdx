## Usage

```tsx
import { SanFrancisco } from "@/components/asciicn/san-francisco";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[480px]">
      <SanFrancisco intensity={0.6} className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1>Your headline</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

The view is from the bay on a grey morning. The Golden Gate Bridge spans the strait: two orange towers with stepped portals between their legs, the main cables sagging between them, a suspender hanging every few columns and a deep truss under the deck. A red light blinks on each tower and cars cross with their lights on. A fog bank rolls in from the ocean side, thickest over the Marin headlands on the left, and pours under the deck while the tower tops stay clear. Behind the south end the city climbs its hills, with the Transamerica Pyramid among the downtown towers and Coit Tower on its hill. Sailboats cross the bay and gulls drift overhead.

The towers move apart as the box gets wider, so a phone screen still shows both of them. With `interactive` on, the default, the layers slide apart as the pointer moves: the water and boats move most, the sky least.

It draws in `standard` glyphs by default. The fog uses light glyphs and the steel uses the densest ones, so the bridge reads in every colour mode. In `truecolor` mode, also the default, the bridge is International Orange over pale blue-grey fog. On a light paper the fog and houses are darkened so they still show. In `mono` mode every glyph takes the ink or a `phosphor`, and in `duotone` mode the scene runs through a `ramp` such as `ember` or `glacier`.

`fog` sets how much of the bridge is buried. At 0 the strait is clear; at 1 the bank covers the deck and climbs the headlands. `intensity` thins every glyph at once. Lower it to around 0.5 under a headline so the text on top stays easy to read.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- With reduced motion turned on, it draws one still frame at `stillTime` and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden. Without WebGL it shows a CSS gradient in the scene's colours.
- The whole scene is drawn on the GPU in one pass. The fog costs the most per sample, and `braille` samples eight times per cell. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
