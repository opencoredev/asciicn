## Usage

```tsx
import { NewYork } from "@/components/asciicn/new-york";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[480px]">
      <NewYork intensity={0.7} className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1>Your headline</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

The view looks at Manhattan from Brooklyn. The Empire State Building rises with its crown floodlit gold and white and a red light blinking on its mast. To its right, the Chrysler Building shows its stacked steel arches and their triangular windows. Behind and around them, thousands of windows switch on and off over time. In front, the Brooklyn Bridge crosses the view on two stone towers with pointed arches, its cables strung with lights and its deck full of traffic. Below, the East River reflects the city and an orange ferry crosses it.

On wide boxes the two landmarks sit right of centre, so a headline fits on the left. On tall boxes they move in and slim down. Moving the pointer slides the near layers further than the far ones.

It draws in `standard` glyphs by default. `braille` gives the finest windows and cables, `blocks` and `dense` solid shapes, and `edge` an outline drawing. In `truecolor`, the default, it paints warm window light on a deep navy. `mono` with a `phosphor` and `duotone` with a `ramp` read the same scene by tone alone.

## Examples

### Behind a headline

`intensity={0.55}` thins the glyphs so white text on the left reads first, and `windows={0.7}` lights more of the skyline to make up for it.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- With reduced motion turned on, it draws one still frame at `stillTime`, with the ferry mid-river, and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden, and releases its WebGL context when far offscreen. Without WebGL it shows a gradient in the same colours.
- The whole city is one shader pass on the GPU. The river reflection reads the skyline a second time, and `braille` samples eight times per cell, so that combination costs the most. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
