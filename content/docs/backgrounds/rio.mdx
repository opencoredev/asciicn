## Usage

```tsx
import { Rio } from "@/components/asciicn/rio";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[480px]">
      <Rio intensity={0.7} className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1>Your headline</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

The Sugarloaf rises from the bay as a granite dome, gold on the side that faces the low sun, with Morro da Urca in front of it. Two cable cars go back and forth: one from the shore to the top of Urca, one from Urca to the summit. Behind them the forested range climbs to Corcovado, where the statue stands with its arms open. Below, the sun lays a path of glitter on the water, rollers break in white lines on the curve of Copacabana, and the promenade's black and white wave mosaic runs along the bottom. Frigatebirds glide overhead and a couple of sailboats cross the bay.

On wide boxes the mountains sit right of centre, so a headline fits on the left. On tall boxes the peaks move toward the middle and draw a little smaller. Moving the pointer slides the near layers further than the far ones.

It draws in `standard` glyphs by default. `braille` gives the finest detail, `blocks` and `dense` solid shapes, and `edge` an outline drawing. In `truecolor`, the default, it paints its own greens, warm stone and sea blue on a deep evening blue. `mono` with a `phosphor` and `duotone` with a `ramp` read the same scene by tone alone.

## Examples

### Behind a headline

`colorMode="duotone"` with the `sodium` ramp turns the scene golden, and `intensity={0.6}` thins the glyphs so white text on the left reads first.

### On a green monitor

`colorMode="mono"` with `phosphor="p1"` draws every glyph in one green. The `braille` charset, an 11px cell and `fx` scanlines, bloom and vignette finish the look, and `surf={0.9}` brings in bigger rollers.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- With reduced motion turned on, it draws one still frame at `stillTime` and stops.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden, and releases its WebGL context when far offscreen. Without WebGL it shows a gradient in the same colours.
- The whole city is one shader pass on the GPU. `braille` samples eight times per cell, so it costs the most. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
