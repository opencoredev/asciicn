## Usage

```tsx
import { Dubai } from "@/components/asciicn/dubai";

export function Hero() {
  return (
    <section className="relative isolate min-h-[480px]">
      <Dubai intensity={0.6} className="absolute inset-0 -z-10" />
      <h1>Your headline</h1>
    </section>
  );
}
```

The city is seen from the lake. At the back are a dusk sky with a low sun and two ridges of dunes. In front of them stand a hazy far skyline, the Burj Khalifa with its setbacks stepping up to the spire, a cluster of glass towers around its base, and the sail of the Burj Al Arab on its island near the shore. The lake below mirrors all of it, broken into ripples.

`hour` sets the time of day. At 0 the sun hangs low over the dunes and the towers glow gold. As `hour` moves toward 1 the sun sets, the line of sunlight climbs the Burj until only the spire holds it, stars come out, windows switch on and floodlights colour the Burj Al Arab. The red beacon on the tip blinks at any hour.

Sand streams off the dune crests, grains blow across the low city, and the air near the ground shimmers in the heat. `sand` sets how much of this you see. Every 14 seconds the fountain at the foot of the Burj runs a show of swaying jets for about seven seconds. With `interactive` on, the pointer shifts the layers by different amounts, so the Burj Al Arab slides further than the dunes.

It draws in `standard` by default. `dense` and `blocks` give the towers more weight, and `braille` keeps the spire and the fountain jets fine. In `truecolor`, the default, glyphs take the scene's gold, sand and dusk blue on a dark dusk paper. In `mono` every glyph takes the theme ink or a `phosphor`, and in `duotone` the tone runs through a `ramp`.

`intensity` scales every glyph's tone. Lower it to keep a headline on top easy to read.

`hour={0.6}` moves the sun down so the sky behind the text is darker, and `intensity={0.7}` pushes the city back.

## Notes

- It is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Put real text on top of it, not inside it.
- With reduced motion turned on, it draws one still frame at `stillTime` and stops. At the default of 2 seconds the fountain is mid-show.
- It pauses when scrolled out of view or when the tab is hidden, and releases its WebGL context when far offscreen. Without WebGL it shows a dusk gradient.
- The scene is one GPU pass with a few short loops (dunes, nearby towers, fountain jets), so its cost depends on the number of samples. `braille` samples eight times per cell; a larger `cell` makes it cheaper. See [Performance](/docs/engine/performance).
